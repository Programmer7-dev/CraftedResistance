Getting started

First launch

The download button does nothing. Why?

Two common causes. Either the build URL has not been published yet, or your browser is blocking the custom protocol. On desktop browsers you can usually allow a site to open an external app from the icon in the address bar.

Why is there no Linux build?

Not yet. Linux support is not on the roadmap at this stage. Join the Discord and it will be announced there first if it changes.

Can I play without installing?

Not at the moment. The craftedresistance:// launch button assumes the desktop build is already installed on your machine.

The game crashes or looks wrong on my machine.

Report it in the Discord with your OS version and GPU. Early builds are rough, and bug reports from testers are the fastest way to get things fixed before release.
