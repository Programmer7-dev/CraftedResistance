Automatic delivery

How it works

No codes, no screenshots and no waiting for a person to check a payment.

1. Open the shop in game

Pause the game and choose SHOP.

Pick the item you want. The game opens a secure Stripe Checkout page in your browser.

2. Pay with Stripe

Card and supported local payment methods.

Stripe handles the payment. The price is decided by the server, so the browser cannot change what is charged.

3. Return to the game

The shop checks for your purchase.

Your item is added to the inventory. If the inventory is full, it drops at your feet instead of disappearing.

One installation at a time. The purchase is tied to the game installation that opened the shop. If you play on another device, open the shop on that device.

Duplicate weapons are flagged. If you already have the weapon in your inventory or in a chest, the shop warns you before charging. You can still choose to buy it.

The free game stays free. Purchases are optional support. They do not unlock the story, the floors or the ending.
